Our Roots

A Family of Guides, a Legacy of Hospitality

Adrar Voyages was founded in 2003 by a family of Amazigh guides in the Draa Valley — a palm-fringed oasis where the Sahara begins. For generations, the Ait Brahim family welcomed travelers into their homes, shared meals around tagine fires, and guided caravans through ancient trade routes.

What began with just three guides and an unshakeable belief in genuine hospitality has grown into a team of over 45 local experts — each one a native storyteller, historian, and cultural ambassador. Two decades of deep local knowledge flow through every journey we craft.

Our philosophy is simple: luxury is not about price, it is about the depth of the experience. We believe the most extraordinary moments cannot be manufactured — they emerge from authentic connection, trust, and a profound respect for the land and its people.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

From a family of three guides in the Draa Valley to Morocco’s most trusted luxury travel operator.

2003
The Beginning

Youssef Ait Brahim and two fellow Amazigh guides launch Adrar Voyages from a small office in Ouarzazate, driven by a vision of tourism that honors local culture.

2008
National Recognition

Awarded the Moroccan National Tourism Prize for Excellence in Cultural Tourism — the first small operator to receive the honor.

2013
A Decade of Trust

Team grows to 20 guides across five regions. Featured in Condé Nast Traveler as one of North Africa’s most authentic luxury operators.

2017
Sustainability Pledge

Launch of the Adrar Foundation, committing 5% of revenue to local education, reforestation, and women’s artisan cooperatives across Morocco.

2021
Global Acclaim

Named in Travel + Leisure’s top luxury operators for Africa and the Middle East. Team surpasses 40 local experts serving guests in 12 regions.

2023
Carbon Neutral Operations

All journeys become fully carbon-neutral through verified offset programs and a partnership with the High Atlas Foundation for tree planting.

2026
Looking Ahead

Carbon neutral certified. 12,000+ trees planted. 45+ local experts. The mission continues — deeper, wider, and more committed than ever to the land and its people.
