Questions & Answers

About the Trip Builder

Our AI analyzes your preferences — interests, travel style, duration, season, group size, and budget — against a database of over 10,000 curated experiences, accommodations, and routes across Morocco. It then generates a personalized day-by-day itinerary that is reviewed and refined by our local expert team.

The AI-generated itinerary is a starting point — think of it as a carefully crafted proposal. You can request modifications to any part of it, and our local travel designers will adjust the route, accommodations, activities, and pacing to perfectly match your preferences.

The AI generates your initial itinerary in under 60 seconds. After that, our local expert team performs a quality review that typically takes 2-4 hours during business hours. You will receive your refined itinerary via email.

The Trip Builder is completely free to use with no obligation. No account is required. Our revenue comes from the travel bookings, not from planning fees.
