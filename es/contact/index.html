All our itineraries include accommodation in hand-picked riads and hotels, private English-speaking guide, ground transportation in comfortable vehicles, curated experiences and excursions, and 24/7 on-trip support. Flights and travel insurance are not included but we can provide recommendations.

Morocco is one of the safest countries in North Africa and welcomes millions of tourists every year. As a local operator, your safety is our top priority. We monitor conditions in real time, our guides are trained in first aid, and we have 24/7 emergency support available throughout your journey.
