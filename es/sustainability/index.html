Our Impact

Travel That
Gives Back

Sustainability isn't a marketing add-on at Adrar Voyages — it's how we operate. Every journey directly funds community projects, environmental restoration, and cultural preservation across Morocco.

Since our founding in 2003, we've believed that extraordinary travel and responsible tourism are not opposing forces — they're inseparable. We source locally, hire exclusively from the communities we visit, offset every gram of trip-related carbon, and channel a percentage of each booking into our Adrar Foundation. The result? Journeys that feel richer for our guests and create lasting, measurable good for the Moroccan people and landscapes we love.

What Guides Us

Our Three Pillars

Every decision we make is filtered through these three commitments.

Community First

We hire locally, pay fair wages above market rate, and reinvest in community projects from education to healthcare.

  • 100% local guides and drivers
  • Fair wages 30% above national average
  • Community health fund since 2018
  • Scholarships for 45 students annually

Environmental Stewardship

From carbon offsetting to reforestation, we work to ensure every trip leaves the landscape better than we found it.

  • 100% carbon offset since 2023
  • 12,000+ native trees planted
  • Zero single-use plastic on all trips
  • Solar-powered desert camps

Cultural Preservation

We partner with artisans, fund heritage restoration, and support language programs to keep Morocco's living culture thriving.

  • Partnerships with 60+ artisan cooperatives
  • Heritage restoration in Fes medina
  • Amazigh language preservation fund
  • Annual craft apprenticeship program
Transparency

Sustainability Scores

Every Adrar trip receives an independently verified sustainability rating. We publish these openly so you know exactly the impact of your journey.

How We Rate Each Trip

Our scoring system evaluates six criteria: local spending ratio, carbon footprint, community employment, waste management, cultural engagement, and environmental restoration contribution. Scores are verified annually by an independent sustainability auditor.

Our Journey

Sustainability Milestones

2019

Adrar Foundation established. Launched our first reforestation project in the Middle Atlas, planting 1,200 cedar saplings with local Amazigh cooperatives.

2020

Zero-waste commitment. Eliminated single-use plastics across all trips and partnered with local recycling initiatives in Marrakech and Fes.

2021

Community health fund. Established healthcare access for 120+ families in remote Atlas villages, covering preventive care and emergency transport.

2022

Sustainability scoring launched. Introduced transparent A+ to C rating system for every trip, verified by independent third-party auditors.

2023

Carbon neutral achieved. All trips became fully carbon offset through verified reforestation and renewable energy projects in Morocco.

2024

Solar-powered desert camps. Transitioned all Sahara camps to 100% solar energy, eliminating diesel generators entirely.

2025

87% local spending milestone. Achieved industry-leading local spend ratio, with 87 dirhams of every 100 spent directly in Moroccan communities.

2026

12,000 trees planted. Reached our reforestation target ahead of schedule. Now launching Phase 2: agroforestry projects with 30 new village partnerships.
