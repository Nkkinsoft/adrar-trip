Questions & Answers

About the Trip Builder

Our AI analyzes your preferences — interests, travel style, duration, season, group size, and budget — against a database of over 10,000 curated experiences, accommodations, and routes across Morocco. It then generates a personalized day-by-day itinerary that is reviewed and refined by our local expert team before being presented to you.

The AI-generated itinerary is a starting point — think of it as a carefully crafted proposal. You can request modifications to any part of it, and our local travel designers will adjust the route, accommodations, activities, and pacing to perfectly match your preferences. There is no limit to revisions.

The AI generates your initial itinerary in under 60 seconds. After that, our local expert team performs a quality review that typically takes 2-4 hours during business hours. You will receive your refined itinerary via email and can also access it in your personal trip dashboard.

The Trip Builder is completely free to use with no obligation. There is no account required to generate your itinerary. If you decide to book, the pricing you see is the final price — no hidden fees, no surprise charges. Our revenue comes from the travel bookings, not from planning fees.
